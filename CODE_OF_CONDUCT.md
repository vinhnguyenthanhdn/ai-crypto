# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other end is trying to build something and is short on time.

## Expected

- Give people the benefit of the doubt, and ask before assuming intent.
- Critique code, numbers and ideas, never the person.
- Back a disagreement with something that can be run: a command, a repro, a measurement.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and that contributors may decline to do more work on one.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, publishing someone's private information, or unwelcome personal attention.

Also not acceptable here: promoting a token, a signal service, a referral link, or a paid group. This repository publishes negative results about its own strategies; it is not a venue for selling anything.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else this project is represented.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue, or privately through the reporting channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if the issue tracker is not appropriate. Maintainers will respond as promptly as they can and may edit, remove, or block as needed. Reports are handled discreetly.
